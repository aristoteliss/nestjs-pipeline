2. PAYMENT TERMS AND REVENUE TIERS
This license requires an annual fee based on Your (or Your organization's) total gross global revenue for the most recently completed fiscal year.

The annual fee is calculated using the following marginal brackets. Each rate applies only to the portion of gross revenue that falls within its bracket, and the annual fee is the sum of the amounts for all brackets:

a) Tier 1: The portion of gross revenue up to $500,000 USD
   Rate: 0% (Free Commercial Use). If gross revenue does not exceed $500,000 USD, the fee is $0.

b) Tier 2: The portion of gross revenue above $500,000 USD and up to $10,000,000 USD
   Rate: 0.1% of that portion.

c) Tier 3: The portion of gross revenue above $10,000,000 USD and up to $50,000,000 USD
   Rate: 0.05% of that portion.

d) Tier 4: The portion of gross revenue above $50,000,000 USD
   Rate: 0.01% of that portion.

The total annual fee never exceeds a maximum hard cap of $50,000 USD per year.

Example: for gross revenue of $20,000,000 USD, the annual fee is 0.1% of $9,500,000 USD ($9,500) plus 0.05% of $10,000,000 USD ($5,000), a total of $14,500 USD.
